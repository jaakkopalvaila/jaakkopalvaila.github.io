Privacy policy

This policy covers the "rclone personal backup" OAuth client described on the home page.

What the app accesses

The app uses the Google Drive API (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) solely to upload, list, verify and download the owner's own files in the owner's own Google Drive.

What is collected, stored or shared

  • No user data is collected by, sent to, or stored by the developer or any third party.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only locally on the owner's own computer, in the rclone configuration file.
  • No data is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone.
  • No data is used for advertising, or to develop, improve or train AI or machine learning models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
